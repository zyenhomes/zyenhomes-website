Check-In Details

Our cleaners will do their very best to prepare our beloved chalet for your group. If anything has been missed, please let us know upon arrival and we will do our best to provide a solution as quickly as possible.

Check-In: After 4:00 PM
Maximum occupancy: 20 guests

Door Access

Keypads are located on both the front and back doors. Your access code will be messaged to you 1 day prior to your arrival date.

Cleanliness & Bedding

All sheets, linens and towels are washed and cleaned prior to arrival. Furniture is disinfected, and door knobs and switches are wiped clean. Cleaning supplies, the vacuum and mop are available in the closet by the front room.

Our bedding includes a mattress protector, fitted sheet, duvet with duvet cover, and pillows with pillow covers. Fitted sheets, duvet covers and pillow covers are freshly washed for each guest arrival. You are welcome to bring your own flat sheet if desired.

BBQ

You are welcome to use the BBQ grill on the patio. Please clean the BBQ properly after use and always turn off the gas valve when finished.

Important House Rules

  • Please do not wear outdoor shoes inside the chalet.
  • No smoking inside the house, by exterior doors, or on the decks. Please remain at least 5–10 feet away from all entrance doors when smoking.
  • Please keep music, TV and other noise low after 11:00 PM. The Town of Blue Mountains strictly enforces its noise bylaws throughout the day. To help avoid disturbing neighbours, please remain inside and keep doors and windows closed when making noise.

Hot Tub / Swimspa

Our Hot Tub/Swimspa is available year-round. No hot tub use after 11:00 PM or before 7:00 AM. Local rules are strictly enforced and violations may result in fines.

  • Everyone must rinse before entering. Sunscreen, lotions and other products can affect the water quality.
  • Lift the left and right covers and place them carefully on the metal cover bar.
  • Please use extra care with the middle cover and place it safely on the deck.
  • Always replace all covers when you are finished.
  • Please bring sandals for the walk between the hot tub and back door. Do not walk barefoot, as salt and dirt can be carried into the hot tub.

Sauna

Our sauna is one of our guests' favourite amenities. Before entering, please read and follow all posted safety instructions and warnings. Enjoy the sauna responsibly and safely.

Garbage & Recycling

Please bag all garbage and recycling and place all bags inside the wooden bin at the front of the house. Please make sure the bin lid is completely closed.
