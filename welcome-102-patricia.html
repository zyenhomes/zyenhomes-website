Door Access

Cleanliness & Bedding

All sheets, linens and towels are washed and cleaned prior to arrival. Furniture is disinfected, and door knobs and switches are wiped clean. Cleaning supplies, the vacuum and mop are available in the closet by the front room.

Our bedding includes a mattress protector, fitted sheet, duvet with duvet cover, and pillows with pillow covers. Fitted sheets, duvet covers and pillow covers are freshly washed for each guest arrival. You are welcome to bring your own flat sheet if desired.

BBQ

You are welcome to use the BBQ grill on the patio. Please clean the BBQ properly after use and always turn off the gas valve when finished.

Important House Rules

  • Please do not wear outdoor shoes inside the chalet.

OUTDOOR COOKING

🔥 Natural Gas BBQ

The BBQ is connected directly to natural gas. The gas shut-off lever is located on the railing behind the BBQ.

  1. Turn Gas ON: Before using the BBQ, turn the gas lever to the ON position.
  2. Use the BBQ: Light and use the BBQ normally.
  3. Turn Everything OFF: After cooking, turn all BBQ burners OFF, then turn the natural gas lever OFF.
  4. Replace the Cover: Allow the BBQ to cool completely, then replace the BBQ cover.
Thank you for double-checking that the gas is turned OFF after every use.
