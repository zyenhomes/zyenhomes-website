WATERFRONT ACTIVITIES

🛶 Water Equipment

Before using any water equipment provided at the cottage, please read the CAUTION menu and follow all equipment-specific instructions.

🦺 Life Jackets

Wear a properly fitted, approved life jacket or personal flotation device while using any watercraft. Check sizing, condition and buckles before each use.

🔍 Before You Go

Inspect equipment for damage, check the weather and water conditions, and ensure everyone knows how to return safely. Never use damaged equipment.

🚣 Responsible Use

Use equipment only for its intended purpose. Do not overload watercraft, and do not use them after drinking alcohol or using impairing substances.

📦 After Use

Bring all equipment back to its designated storage area and secure it so it cannot drift away.

Contact your host if you are unsure what equipment is available or how to use it.

IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

⚠️ CAUTION — WATERFRONT SAFETY

WATERFRONT HAZARD — PLEASE READ BEFORE ENTERING THE WATER
Water depth, currents, underwater obstacles and weather conditions can change unexpectedly. Entering the water and using water equipment involves risk of serious injury or drowning.

👶 Children Must Be Supervised

Never leave children unattended near the water, shoreline or dock, even briefly. An adult must actively supervise children at all times.

🦺 Wear a Life Jacket

Wear a properly fitted, approved life jacket or personal flotation device when using any watercraft. Do not rely on swimming ability alone.

🚫 No Diving or Jumping

Do not dive or jump from the dock or shoreline. Water may be shallow or contain submerged hazards.

⛈️ Weather & Water Conditions

Stay out of the water during thunderstorms, strong winds, poor visibility or unsafe conditions. Never enter the water alone.

🍺 No Impaired Water Activities

Do not swim or operate watercraft while impaired by alcohol or drugs.

🧊 Winter Ice

Do not walk or skate on frozen water. Ice thickness and safety cannot be assumed.

🆘 Emergency

In an emergency, call 911 immediately and provide the address: 940 Seven Oaks Drive, Otonabee-South Monaghan, Ontario.
