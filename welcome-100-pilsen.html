Door Access

Cleanliness & Bedding

All sheets, linens and towels are washed and cleaned prior to arrival. Furniture is disinfected, and door knobs and switches are wiped clean. Cleaning supplies, the vacuum and mop are available in the closet by the front room.

Our bedding includes a mattress protector, fitted sheet, duvet with duvet cover, and pillows with pillow covers. Fitted sheets, duvet covers and pillow covers are freshly washed for each guest arrival. You are welcome to bring your own flat sheet if desired.

Towels

We provide 1 hand towel per bathroom.

We provide 1–2 dish cloths for the kitchen.

BBQ

You are welcome to use the BBQ grill on the patio. Please clean the BBQ properly after use and always turn off the gas valve when finished.

Important House Rules

  • Please do not wear outdoor shoes inside the chalet.
  • No smoking inside the house or near exterior doors. Please remain at least 5–10 feet away from all entrance doors when smoking.
  • Please keep music, TV and other noise low after 11:00 PM. The Town of Blue Mountains strictly enforces its noise bylaws throughout the day. To help avoid disturbing neighbours, please remain inside and keep doors and windows closed when making noise.

Hot Tub

Our Hot Tub is available year-round. No hot tub use after 11:00 PM or before 7:00 AM. Local rules are strictly enforced and violations may result in fines.

  • Everyone must rinse before entering. Sunscreen, lotions and other products can affect the water quality.
  • Always replace all covers when you are finished.
  • Please bring sandals for the walk between the hot tub and back door. Do not walk barefoot, as salt and dirt can be carried into the hot tub.

Garbage & Recycling

Please bag all garbage and recycling and place all bags inside the large bin at the back of the property. Please make sure the bin lid is completely closed.

House Rules

To ensure everyone has a safe and enjoyable stay, and to respect our neighbours, please follow these house rules:

Please keep music, TVs and other noise at a respectful level, especially after 11:00 PM.

RELAX & ENJOY

♨️ Hot Tub

Our Hot Tub is available year-round.

⏰ Hours of Use

No Hot Tub use after 11:00 PM or before 7:00 AM. Local rules are strictly enforced, and violations may result in fines.

🚿 Please Rinse Before Entering

Everyone must rinse before entering. Sunscreen, lotions and other products can affect the water quality.

🛁 Hot Tub Cover

Carefully remove the hot tub cover before use and replace it securely when finished.

Always replace all covers after every use. Do not sit, stand or place objects on the covers.

🩴 Please Wear Sandals

Please bring sandals for walking between the Hot Tub and the back door. Do not walk barefoot, as salt and dirt can be carried into the water.

⚠️ Do Not Adjust Settings
Do not adjust the temperature or any system settings. Please contact us if you need assistance.

🥤 No Food or Drinks

Food and drinks are not permitted inside the Hot Tub.

Thank you for helping us keep the Hot Tub clean, safe and enjoyable for everyone.

COZY & COMFORTABLE

🔥 Fireplace

The small blue flame (pilot light) should remain on at all times. Please do not turn off the pilot light.

To operate the fireplace, use the switch located on the middle wall post.

⚠️ Hot Glass Warning

The fireplace glass can become extremely hot during use and may remain hot after the fireplace is turned off. Please keep children and pets away from the fireplace to prevent burns.

OUTDOOR COOKING

🔥 Propane BBQ

The BBQ is connected to a propane tank. The gas shut-off lever is located on the propane tank.

  1. Turn Gas ON: Before using the BBQ, turn the gas lever to the ON position.
  2. Use the BBQ: Light and use the BBQ normally, following the BBQ manufacturer's lighting instructions.
  3. Turn Everything OFF: After cooking, turn all BBQ burners OFF, then turn the valve on the propane tank OFF.
  4. Replace the Cover: Allow the BBQ to cool completely, then replace the BBQ cover.
Thank you for double-checking that the gas is turned OFF after every use.
