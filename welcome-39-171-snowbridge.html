⚠️ Sauna Safety

PLEASE READ BEFORE USING THE SAUNA

For your safety and enjoyment, all guests must follow these rules.

🌡️ Temperature & Time Limit

  • Limit each sauna session to 10–15 minutes, especially if you are not accustomed to sauna use.
  • Never exceed 20 minutes per session.
  • Do not adjust the sauna's maximum temperature settings.
  • Exit immediately if you feel dizzy, weak, nauseous, or uncomfortable.

💧 Stay Hydrated

  • Drink plenty of water before and after using the sauna.
  • No alcohol or drugs before or during sauna use.
  • Never use the sauna while intoxicated.

❤️ Health & Medical Warnings

  • Guests who are pregnant or have heart conditions, high or low blood pressure, or other medical concerns should consult a healthcare professional before using the sauna.
  • Do not use the sauna if you are feeling unwell.
  • Never fall asleep inside the sauna.

👨‍👩‍👧 Children & Supervision

  • Children must be supervised by a responsible adult at all times.
  • Young children should not use the sauna.
  • Never use the sauna alone.

🚫 Sauna Safety & Proper Use

  • Do not touch the sauna heater, stones, or other hot surfaces.
  • Do not place towels, clothing, or any objects on or near the heater.
  • Do not pour water, oils, or other liquids onto the heater unless specifically permitted by the host.
  • No food, glass containers, smoking, or vaping inside the sauna.
  • Do not hang wet clothing or towels inside the sauna.
  • Keep the sauna door closed during use.

OUTDOOR COOKING

🔥 Natural Gas BBQ

The BBQ is connected directly to natural gas. The gas shut-off lever is located on the wall beside the BBQ.

  1. Turn Gas ON: Before using the BBQ, turn the gas lever to the ON position.
  2. Use the BBQ: Light and use the BBQ normally, following the BBQ manufacturer's lighting instructions.
  3. Turn Everything OFF: After cooking, turn all BBQ burners OFF, then turn the natural gas lever OFF.
  4. Replace the Cover: Allow the BBQ to cool completely, then replace the BBQ cover.
Thank you for double-checking that the gas is turned OFF after every use.
